High Roller is an extravagant black-based purple nail polish with eye-catching magnetic pigment that effortlessly shifts from purple to pink to copper.

Take your nail game to the next level with High Roller, a sultry black-based magnetic that exudes the charm of a high-stakes gambler. Stop and stare as a stunning magnetic effect shifts seamlessly from sultry purple to dazzling pink and fiery copper, capturing the excitement of an all-or-nothing night out. Turn heads, make a statement, and take a chance with High Roller.

High Roller is part of the Nightlife Collection, a 6-piece collection where color comes to life at night under the glowing neon lights and the sparkling cityscape. Embrace the exhilarating spirit of Nightlife where bold, bright, and colorful magnetic hues light up against a blackened base for an effect that is nothing short of electrifying!

We recommend 2-3 coats for the ideal finish
